I learned docker, a tool for containerizing applications and turning them into microservices. I also learned AppMap, a tool for capturing and analyzing the dynamic behaviour of a JVM application.

Reflect

I did not have a lot to do for the first week so it was really easy for me to get the job done. For the first week I think I did a very good job. The diagrams I used did not use AppMap so I had to do them again in the second week. I learned that I can easily forget my tasks if I do not write them down.

Reflection on Learning Objectives

How well did I meet my learning objectives?

I think I learned how to use the feedback I receive to improve the quality of my work. This skill can always be improved further but I believe that I have improved it to a good degree. This is evident by the fact that my supervisor was satisfied with my work after I implemented his advice.

As for learning palladio, I am done with the basics and I am able to run simulations and make performance models. The tools had many other features that I have not learned and do not need to learn for my research purpose. So I think I have learned palladio really well.

As for learning scientific research and how to write abstracts, I believe that I have some idea on how to collect objective evidence and analyze it, but there is still much room left for improvement. Additionally, I really have not started working on my abstract. Therefore, I have not met this objective yet.

Objectives not fully met

I have not really met my research and abstract writing goal since I am in the first half of my placement. I plan to focus on these skills for the remainder of my placement and ask my supervisor for guidance.

Most important new skills learned

The most important skills I learned are soft skills such as learning to improve with feedback, learning to present my progress, and finally learning how to work independently and manage my own time.

Personal growth to carry forward

I have learned many different skills and got to know myself at a deeper level during this placement. I learned how to spot my flaws and quickly adapt so I can improve my work quality, I believe this is the most important bit of personal development that I can carry into my future.
